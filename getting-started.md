## What You'll Learn
- Set up your DocuSphere account.
- Navigate the user interface.
- Create and share documents.
- Collaborate with team members in real time.

## Prerequisites
- A valid email address for account registration.
- Access to the internet.
- Supported browsers: Google Chrome (latest version), Mozilla Firefox (latest version), or Microsoft Edge (latest version).

## Getting Started with DocuSphere

1. **Visit the DocuSphere Website**  
   Open your preferred web browser and go to [DocuSphere](https://www.docusphere.com).

2. **Create an Account**  
   Click on the "Sign Up" button located on the homepage. Fill in your email address and create a password. Confirm your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.

3. **Log In to Your Account**  
   Return to the DocuSphere homepage and click "Log In." Enter your credentials to access your workspace.

4. **Familiarize Yourself with the Dashboard**  
   Once logged in, take a moment to explore the dashboard. Notice the navigation menu on the left, which includes options for creating documents, accessing shared files, and managing your account settings.

5. **Create a New Document**  
   Click on the "New Document" button at the top right of the dashboard. Choose from various templates or start with a blank document. Use the rich text editor to format your content.

6. **Share Your Document**  
   After creating your document, click the "Share" button. Enter the email addresses of your team members and set their permissions (view or edit). Click "Send" to notify them.

7. **Collaborate in Real Time**  
   Invite your team to edit the document simultaneously. Use the comments feature to discuss changes and suggestions. Track revisions using the version history option.

8. **Organize Your Documents**  
   Create folders by clicking on the "New Folder" button. Drag and drop documents into these folders for better organization. Use tags to categorize documents for easier searching.

9. **Explore Integrations**  
   Navigate to the "Integrations" section in the settings menu. Connect DocuSphere with tools like Slack, Trello, or Google Calendar to enhance your workflow.

10. **Access Help Resources**  
    If you encounter any issues, visit the Help Center by clicking on the question mark icon in the top right corner. Browse FAQs or submit a support ticket for assistance.

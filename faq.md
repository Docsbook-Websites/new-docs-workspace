## How does SchemaType ensure data privacy?

SchemaType prioritizes data privacy by following industry-standard security practices. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not store your database credentials or any sensitive information. You maintain full control over your database schemas and typed clients.

## How does SchemaType differ from Prisma, TypeORM, and Hasura?

SchemaType uniquely generates typed clients directly from Postgres schemas, enhancing type safety and reducing errors. Unlike Prisma and TypeORM, which require additional configuration, SchemaType automates client generation based on your existing schema. Hasura focuses on GraphQL APIs, while SchemaType is specifically designed for typed client generation, making it ideal for developers seeking type safety in SQL-based applications.

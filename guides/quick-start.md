# How to Create and Collaborate on Documents in DocuSphere

Follow these steps to efficiently create and collaborate on documents using DocuSphere. This guide will help you leverage the platform's features for optimal teamwork.

## Step 1: Sign Up or Log In

1. Navigate to the DocuSphere website.
2. Click on the "Sign Up" button if you are a new user, or "Log In" if you already have an account.
3. Complete the registration process or enter your credentials to access your workspace.

## Step 2: Create a New Document

1. Once logged in, locate the "Documents" tab on the left sidebar.
2. Click on the "New Document" button at the top right corner.
3. Choose a document type from the dropdown menu (e.g., Blank Document, Template).
4. Enter a title for your document and click "Create."

## Step 3: Use the Editor

1. Familiarize yourself with the editing interface.
2. Utilize the formatting toolbar to style your text, add headings, and insert lists.
3. Insert images, tables, or links by using the "Insert" menu.
4. Save your work regularly by clicking the "Save" button or enabling auto-save in settings.

## Step 4: Invite Collaborators

1. Click on the "Share" button located in the top right corner of the document.
2. Enter the email addresses of your team members in the "Invite Collaborators" field.
3. Set permissions for each collaborator (e.g., Viewer, Editor).
4. Click "Send Invites" to notify your team.

## Step 5: Collaborate in Real-Time

1. Once your collaborators accept the invitation, they can join the document.
2. Use the chat feature on the right sidebar to discuss changes or ideas in real-time.
3. Track changes made by collaborators through the "Version History" feature.
4. Resolve comments and suggestions directly within the document for clarity.

## Step 6: Finalize and Export Your Document

1. Review the document for any final edits or adjustments.
2. Click on the "Export" button to choose your desired file format (e.g., PDF, DOCX).
3. Select the export settings and click "Download" to save the document to your device.
